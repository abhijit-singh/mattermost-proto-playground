- Suggestion: stale attribute shown through `Select` `placeholder` with a `value` that is not in `options` (`PolicyDetailPage.tsx:129-131`). The control is meant to show a selected option. `FieldMessage` was the frame's recommendation.
- Suggestion: `SectionNotice type="info"` with `role="status"` carries "Clearance deleted." (`AttributeManagementPage.tsx:76-83`). The choose-this-not-that table says transient confirmation uses `Toast`, not `SectionNotice`. It is defensible here because the notice persists and carries an action, so state that reason in a comment.
- Suggestion: `Tag type="warning"` is white on amber (`PolicyDetailPage.tsx:147-152`, `PolicyListPage.tsx:78-82`). The contrast could not be verified without a browser.
- Not found: root-barrel imports, raw hex or ms values, inline styles, `!important`, missing IconButton labels, a second primary Button, a restyled cousin, or invented chrome. Local compositions have headers.
